WELCOME

Heartbeats Percussion Studio provides individualized beginner percussion instruction for children ages 5 through 10. Lessons are taught by Bill Fritz in a structured home-studio environment. A parent or legal guardian remains present throughout every lesson.

This packet explains the studio’s launch policies in plain language. Completing the registration form confirms that the parent or legal guardian has reviewed and accepted these policies.

1. LESSON OFFER AND PRICING

New students complete one-time registration and their first lesson request together in the New Student Registration & Lesson Request form. Standard lesson choices and pricing apply from the first appointment onward: $30 for 30 minutes, $45 for 45 minutes, or $60 for 60 minutes.

Registered students may request any of these pay-per-lesson options:

  • 30-minute lesson — $30
  • 45-minute lesson — $45
  • 60-minute lesson — $60

Each lesson is paid separately. There is no subscription or lesson bundle. Families may request one lesson at a time or a recurring weekly time. Pricing applies per student and per lesson. Each sibling registers separately, has a separate student record, and has separate lesson requests and payment records. Sibling lessons may be scheduled back-to-back for convenience.

2. ELIGIBILITY AND LESSON FORMAT

Public enrollment is initially limited to children ages 5 through 10.

Lessons are normally one student at a time. Instruction may include practice pad, drum set, keyboard, drumsticks, rhythm exercises, and hand percussion. Activities are adapted to the student’s age, attention, coordination, experience, and instructional needs.

Heartbeats provides music instruction. It does not provide childcare, therapy, medical treatment, behavioral-health treatment, or special-education services.

3. REGISTRATION AND BOOKING

A parent or legal guardian completes the New Student Registration & Lesson Request form for each new student. The registration portion remains on file and needs to be updated only when information changes.

The same new-student submission includes the first lesson request. A request is not a confirmed appointment. Bill reviews the student information, requested lesson length, and preferred time, and normally responds within a few hours during business days.

After Bill approves the requested lesson length and an available time, Square sends the applicable $30 / $45 / $60 payment request; the appointment is confirmed after payment is received.

The home lesson address is not posted publicly. For a new family, it is provided privately only after registration is verified, payment for the approved lesson is received, and Bill confirms the appointment.

4. SCHEDULING

Regular teaching hours are weekdays between 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM. On Wednesdays, lessons begin no earlier than 12:30 PM.

Families may request lessons up to 30 days in advance. Recurring weekly students may reserve beyond the 30-day window.

A lesson may be requested as little as one hour before the desired start time. Requests made less than three hours in advance are considered short-notice requests and remain subject to instructor availability. Every short-notice lesson still requires Bill's approval and the applicable payment before confirmation.

Lessons may be scheduled back-to-back. Arrivals should be timed so only one family is entering or leaving the lesson area at a time.

5. PAYMENT

Every lesson is paid individually through Square after the lesson length/time is approved and before that lesson is confirmed. Standard rates are $30 / $45 / $60 for 30 / 45 / 60 minutes. Automatic recurring billing is not enabled.

Accepted payment methods may include card, cash, Cash App, Venmo, and Zelle. Personal checks are not accepted.

Square serves as the studio’s appointment and payment record. Cash and other off-platform payments are still entered into Square so appointments, credits, refunds, and payments are consistently documented.

6. CANCELLATIONS AND RESCHEDULING

For any paid appointment, the first solution for a family cancellation is to move the appointment to another available date and time.

When rescheduling is not possible:

The reschedule-first paid-lesson cancellation policy applies consistently to every lesson.

For any paid continuing lesson, any credit or refund is handled under the paid-lesson cancellation policy and recorded in Square.

For paid lessons, families may request a reschedule and Heartbeats will make a reasonable effort to move the lesson to another available time. If a paid lesson cannot reasonably be rescheduled, $15 of that lesson payment is retained as a future student credit and the remaining paid balance is refunded when reasonably possible. A no-show without notice normally forfeits the lesson payment. If Heartbeats cancels, the family may transfer the full payment to another available lesson or receive a full refund.

Credits are assigned to the individual student and are recorded in Square. Credits are not redeemable for cash.

If Bill cancels a lesson, the family may choose either:

  • Transfer of the full payment to another available lesson date and time; or
  • A full refund to the original payment method.

7. LATE ARRIVALS

A late arrival does not extend the lesson. The appointment ends at the originally scheduled time so later appointments remain on schedule.

The scheduled lesson credit or applicable charge still applies when the family arrives late.

8. ILLNESS

Students and accompanying adults must reschedule when they have:

  • Fever;
  • Repeated vomiting;
  • Significant diarrhea; or
  • Symptoms severe enough to prevent normal participation.

A student or accompanying adult may return when respiratory symptoms have been improving overall for at least 24 hours and the person has been fever-free for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medication.

After vomiting or diarrhea, the person should be symptom-free for at least 24 hours before returning.

Illness-related cancellations follow the normal rescheduling-first cancellation policy.

9. PARENT OR GUARDIAN PRESENCE

A parent or legal guardian must remain on the premises for the entire lesson and stay in the same room as the student and instructor.

Drop-off lessons are not offered. The accompanying adult remains responsible for the child during the visit, including restroom needs, personal care, medication, and emergency decisions.

10. HOME-STUDIO ARRIVAL AND ACCESS

Lessons are by appointment only. The studio does not accept walk-ins.

There is no outdoor waiting area and no exterior business signage. Families should arrive close to the scheduled time and follow the parking and arrival directions provided with the confirmed appointment.

Only the registered student and the accompanying parent or legal guardian should attend unless Bill has approved another arrangement in advance.

No pets, other children, household members, roommates, or visitors will have access to the lesson area while a student is present.

Families have a clear route from parking to the lesson room and may access a designated restroom without entering private areas of the home.

11. INSTRUMENT USE AND HEARING COMFORT

Students are expected to follow instructor directions for responsible instrument use. Activities may involve acoustic drums and other instruments capable of producing loud sounds.

Bill will use age-appropriate volume controls, technique instruction, practice pads, muffling, instrument selection, breaks, and other reasonable methods to maintain a productive environment.

A parent should inform Bill before the lesson about sound sensitivity, hearing concerns, sensory needs, physical restrictions, or other considerations that may affect participation.

12. STUDENT EQUIPMENT AND HOME PRACTICE

Students will initially need a practice pad and drumsticks for home practice. Access to a drum set may become helpful later but is not required at the start.

Heartbeats may offer a standardized beginner practice kit through prepaid special order. No family is required to buy equipment from Heartbeats. Exact products, price, sales tax, pickup, and return terms will be disclosed before payment.

13. PRIVATE INSTRUCTIONAL PHOTOGRAPHY, VIDEO, AND AUDIO

Media permission is optional. Declining any media permission does not affect lesson eligibility, lesson availability, pricing, instruction, or participation.

The registration form allows the parent or legal guardian to separately approve or decline:

  • Private instructional photography;
  • Private instructional video or audio recording;

Private instructional media may be used to review technique, posture, grip, rhythm, timing, coordination, progress, lesson planning, or private home-practice guidance. Private instructional permission does not authorize public or promotional use.

Heartbeats will not use student photos, video, or audio for marketing, advertising, social media, flyers, brochures, posters, presentations, the public website, community-event promotion, or any other public or promotional purpose.

No public student-identification choice is collected because student media is not authorized for publication.

Private instructional media is handled as restricted student-record material and is not intended for public distribution.

Parents may record reasonable portions of their own child’s lesson for private home-practice reference when the recording does not disrupt instruction, include another student or family, or disclose another person without permission.

14. COMMUNICATION AND RECORDS

The studio may communicate with the registering parent or legal guardian about registration, lesson requests, approval, payment, reminders, cancellations, rescheduling, practice notes, equipment, and future lesson availability.

Parents are responsible for keeping contact, medical, instructional, and emergency information current.

Student records and private instructional media are handled as restricted operational records and are not intended for public distribution. Heartbeats does not authorize student media for public/promotional use. The secure student record stores only the current optional private instructional photography and private instructional video/audio permission values needed for instructional operations.

15. INSTRUCTOR INFORMATION

Bill Fritz is the owner and instructor. He has current Scouting America Youth Protection Training and a Florida Level 2 background screening through the Alachua County school system, with documentation maintained by the studio.

These credentials should not be interpreted as government licensing of the studio or as a substitute for parent supervision.

Bill does not currently represent that he holds active CPR or First Aid certification.

16. SAFETY AND EMERGENCIES

The lesson area has a working smoke alarm, an accessible fire extinguisher, and a stocked first-aid kit nearby.

Because the parent or legal guardian remains present, that adult remains responsible for emergency decisions involving the student. Nothing in the registration form prevents any person from contacting emergency services when circumstances reasonably require it.

17. POLICY CHANGES

Heartbeats may revise policies as the studio develops. Material changes will be provided in writing before they apply to a future booking.

A policy version already accepted for a confirmed lesson will not be retroactively changed for that lesson unless the change is necessary for immediate safety, legal compliance, or mutual agreement.

18. QUESTIONS AND ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Families should ask questions before signing the registration form or paying for a lesson.

By completing the New Student Registration & Lesson Request form, the parent or legal guardian acknowledges receiving, reviewing, understanding, and accepting the current Parent Policy Packet.
