Four simple steps

How registration and booking work

Submit one request, then wait for approval. You are not charged when you send the form.

  1. 1

    New families register and request in one form

    Complete the New Student Registration & Lesson Request form, choose 30, 45, or 60 minutes, and request a preferred day and time.

  2. 2

    Bill reviews the request

    Bill reviews the student information, lesson length, and preferred time, then approves an available appointment.

  3. 3

    Pay only after approval

    Heartbeats sends the applicable $30, $45, or $60 Square payment request only after the lesson length and time are approved.

  4. 4

    Confirmation completes the booking

    After payment is received and recorded, Bill confirms the appointment. A new family then receives the private studio address and arrival information.

Returning family? Registered families use the Lesson Request form for later lessons. Individual lessons and recurring weekly times follow the same approval → payment → confirmation sequence.

Parent information

Questions parents usually ask

Clear answers about scheduling, supervision, equipment, cancellations, illness, and privacy — before you request a lesson.

Where and when are lessons offered?

Lessons are held by appointment in a controlled home-studio environment in Gainesville, Florida. The private address is provided only after payment is recorded and Bill formally accepts and confirms the appointment.

Hours are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday 11:00 AM–5:00 PM; Wednesday 12:30 PM–5:00 PM. No walk-ins.

Can I drop my child off?

No. A parent or legal guardian remains on the premises and in the lesson room for the entire lesson. The adult remains responsible for medication, personal care, restroom assistance, supervision, and emergency decisions.

Does my child need prior music experience or a drum set?

No prior music experience is required. A student does not need to own equipment before the first lesson unless Bill gives a specific recommendation. Students who continue will generally need a practice pad and drumsticks.

How do siblings and recurring lessons work?

Each sibling is a separate student with separate registration, lesson charges, and records. Lessons may be scheduled back-to-back. Families may request a recurring weekly time; payment remains lesson by lesson.

What is the cancellation and late-arrival policy?

Rescheduling comes first. If rescheduling is not possible, $15 remains non-refundable as a credit toward the same student’s next lesson and the remaining paid balance is refunded to the original payment method when reasonably possible.

A no-show without notice normally forfeits the lesson payment. If Bill cancels, the family may choose a full payment transfer or a full refund. Late arrival does not extend the lesson.

When should we reschedule for illness?

Please reschedule for fever, repeated vomiting, significant diarrhea, or symptoms severe enough to prevent normal participation. Return when respiratory symptoms have been improving overall for at least 24 hours and the student has been fever-free for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medication. After vomiting or diarrhea, return when symptom-free for at least 24 hours.

Will my child be photographed or recorded?

Only two separate optional studio permissions are used: private instructional photography and private instructional video/audio. Declining either or leaving either unanswered does not affect eligibility, pricing, instruction, participation, or booking access.

Heartbeats does not use student photos, video, or audio for marketing, advertising, social media, flyers, the public website, community-event promotion, or other public/promotional purposes.
